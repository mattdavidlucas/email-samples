Welcome to Human Insight Quarterly (HiQ), UserTesting’s newsletter curated specifically for the analyst community. In today’s climate, we’re all having to adapt to new ways of doing business. It’s about being able to pivot, embrace change, and design new ways of doing traditional things like, in-person briefings that we looked forward to during the year. Despite not being able to see you all in person, we still felt it was important to keep you up-to-date on our business; platform adoption, trends, research findings, product launches, partnerships, and other interesting insights we’ve synthesized over the past few months.

As a remote feedback solution, we’ve been able to help even more companies with their research needs during COVID–giving them accessibility to test templates and audiences to gather insights in a safe and virtual way. UserTesting’s ability to deliver insights remotely, and on-demand, influenced a strong first half of the year. We brought on 272 new logos globally and saw an increase in testing on the platform, which is the case as more companies have an increased need to re-learn their customers, now, more than ever before.

In what we hope is an easy to consume, and digestible format, here is what UserTesting has been up to over the last six months.
